Harry Potter and the Goblet of Fire follows Harry during his fourth year at Hogwarts, where he unexpectedly becomes one of the champions in the dangerous Triwizard Tournament. He must compete in three challenging tasks against older students from other wizarding schools while dealing with growing tensions in the wizarding world. As the tournament progresses, Harry discovers that someone has secretly manipulated the competition for a much darker purpose. The story ends with the return of Lord Voldemort, marking a major turning point in Harry's fight against him.
